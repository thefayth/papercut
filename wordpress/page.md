# Papercut

Public surface. Private engine. Receipts always.

Papercut is a review-first paperwork operations system for recurring forms,
packets, signatures, portals, email, fax, workflow memory, and dispatch
receipts.

It helps people and teams prepare complicated administrative work without
losing the human approval edge. The public story is visible. The private app,
records, credentials, and operational engine stay protected.

## What This Project Is

Papercut is a paperwork command center. It brings document intake, packet prep,
review copies, private profile memory, portal runbooks, provider readiness, and
follow-up history into one workflow.

It is built for work where "almost submitted" is not the same as submitted, and
where proof matters.

## Why It Matters

Paperwork failures are often system failures:

- a form was uploaded but not confirmed
- a fax was sent but no proof was saved
- a portal status changed but nobody checked
- a signature was reused without a clean review copy
- a deadline moved from memory to panic

Papercut turns those fragile handoffs into queues, review gates, provider
checks, and receipts.

## How It Works

1. Bring in a form, packet, portal task, email/fax destination, or recurring
   workflow.
2. Papercut organizes the work inside a private operator queue.
3. The operator reviews the packet, destination, readiness, and blockers.
4. Approved work can be staged for email, fax, portal upload, status check, ATS
   apply, or worker dispatch.
5. Live actions remain gated by credentials, review mode, and per-item approval.
6. Receipts and follow-up status stay connected to the workflow.
